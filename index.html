LEARN & PROTECT

Security Awareness Center

Learn about common cyber threats and prevention.

🎣 Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to steal sensitive information.

Prevention:

Verify senders and avoid suspicious links.

🦠 Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices or access information.

Prevention:

Use trusted software and keep devices updated.

🔒 Ransomware

Malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment.

Prevention:

Keep offline backups and avoid unknown attachments.

🔑 Two-Factor Authentication

An extra verification step to protect your accounts.

Prevention:

Enable 2FA and use an authenticator app where possible.

🌐 Safe Browsing

Safe practices while visiting websites and using online services.

Prevention:

Check domains and avoid suspicious downloads.

🛡️ Privacy Protection

Protect personal information and digital identity.

Prevention:

Limit public information and review privacy settings.
